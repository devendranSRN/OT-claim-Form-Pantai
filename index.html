Notes:
• Senior Executives, Executives, Officers and Supervisors are not entitled to claim overtime.
• All overtime must be endorsed by HODs. Claims must reach Payroll/HRD before 6th of each month.
• OT Rules: <45 min = 0 hrs. 45–89 min = 1 hr. 90–104 min = 1.5 hrs. ≥105 min rounds by 0.5 hr.
• Working hours: Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 | Sat 09:00–13:00 | Sun = Rest Day.
• TOIL: Selecting an OT date reference automatically adjusts the "OT Permitted To" end time. A purple field means it has been adjusted by TOIL.
• All times in 24-hour format — enter as HH:MM (e.g. 17:00, 21:30).
